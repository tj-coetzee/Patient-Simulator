The BioSim Tech Patient Simulator project seeks to transform medical education by tackling the critical issue of cost-effective, programmable patient simulators. 
Current market solutions are frequently too expensive, rendering them inaccessible to smaller medical facilities and educational programs. 
Furthermore, most simulations lack the adaptability essential for efficient medical teaching, limiting their use in a variety of settings. 
BioSim Tech understands the importance of providing healthcare workers with cheap and adaptive tools for dealing with a wide range of patient conditions and emergencies. 
The patient simulator offers a cost-effective solution without compromising on functionality. 
By mimicking ECG and EMG data and allowing users to input their own waveforms, the simulator provides a versatile platform for medical training.
